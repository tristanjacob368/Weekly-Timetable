Five-session split

Gym

Chest

Rules

  • Run sessions in order, not by weekday. If a day is missed, the next session is next in line.
  • Stop 1–2 reps before failure on compounds. Go to failure on the last set of isolations.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight.
  • Rest 2–3 min on heavy lifts and 60–90 sec on the rest.
  • On shift days, train late morning or early afternoon and take Chest, Back or Upper. Put legs on days off.
  • Eat about 300 kcal over maintenance with 100–130g protein a day.
  • Run it for 6–8 weeks before changing anything.
